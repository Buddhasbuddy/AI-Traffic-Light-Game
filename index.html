Before You Prompt — For Out-of-Scope Leaders

For each proposed use, decide whether using Copilot this way is generally appropriate, needs caution, or should stop until the information, purpose, and authority are confirmed.

Green — Generally appropriate: The task is a good fit for Copilot and can be completed with public, generic, synthetic, or appropriately non-sensitive information. Normal human review still applies.

Yellow — Proceed thoughtfully: The use may be appropriate, but it involves internal work, interpretation, consequential decisions, or information that should be minimized or de-identified. Confirm the purpose, authority, governing source, and level of human review.

Red — Stop and confirm: As proposed, the use requires restricted, highly sensitive, identifiable, confidential, or security-related information, or a use for which permission has not been established. Do not proceed as written until the tool, information, and purpose are explicitly approved.

Assume: Unless a scenario says otherwise, the user is signed into Saskatchewan Polytechnic's approved Microsoft 365 Copilot environment. The Traffic Light evaluates whether the use is appropriate — not whether Copilot's eventual answer will be correct.
